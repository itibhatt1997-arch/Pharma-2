About us

Built on science, quality and responsible care

Starus Pharma is an ISO 9001:2015 certified, professionally managed pharmaceutical group headquartered in Hyderabad, India. We focus on chronic and specialized therapy areas, delivering innovative and meaningful healthcare solutions.

Since inception, we have concentrated on identifying evolving healthcare needs and building a strong presence in selected therapeutic areas. Our philosophy is built around scientific knowledge, quality, innovation, customer relationships and responsible business practices.

We seek to create long-term value for patients, healthcare professionals, business partners, employees and other stakeholders by continuously strengthening our capabilities and expanding our healthcare portfolio.

  • ISO 9001:2015 Certified

Starus Division

Chronic & specialized therapies

The growing prevalence of chronic diseases has created an increasing need for effective, accessible and innovative healthcare solutions. Conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, arthritis, gastrointestinal disorders, neurological disorders and psychiatric conditions continue to place significant demands on healthcare systems and communities.

Recognizing these evolving requirements, Starus Pharma established its focused Starus Division to address selected chronic and specialized therapy areas. With focused leadership and a commitment to scientific and market knowledge, the division continues to strengthen its capabilities.

  • Diabetes
  • Hypertension
  • Arthritis
  • GI disorders
  • Neurological
  • Psychiatric

The division is committed to

  • Developing and introducing differentiated healthcare solutions.
  • Strengthening its presence across key chronic therapy segments.
  • Working closely with physicians and healthcare professionals.
  • Building scientific and therapeutic expertise.
  • Continuously identifying opportunities for innovation.
  • Supporting healthcare professionals with reliable and meaningful therapeutic solutions.

Starus Foundation & CSR

Creating value beyond business

The Starus Foundation represents our commitment to a positive, sustainable impact on society. We support communities through education, healthcare, livelihood enhancement and environmental sustainability — creating opportunities and improving access to essential resources.

Education

Supporting access to education, skill development, vocational training and opportunities for learning and professional growth — including government schools, digital learning and partnerships with training institutions.

Healthcare

Environment
